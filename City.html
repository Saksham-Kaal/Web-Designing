Greater Noida:

Greater Noida is a planned city in Uttar Pradesh, India.
It is near Delhi and is famous for its colleges, wide roads and green areas.

India Expo Centre and Mart:

This is one of the biggest exhibition centres in India.
Many trade fairs and big events like the Auto Expo are held here.

Knowledge Parks:

This area consist of several colleges and Universities.
It is a Educational Hub for the city Greater Noida.

Pari Chowk:

Pari Chowk is a famous roundabout in the middle of the city.
It is named after the fairy (pari) statue in the centre.
